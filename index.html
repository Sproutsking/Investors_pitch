11The Equilibrium Principle

A closed system.
Always resolves to 100%.

If a campaign creates a creator pool, that pool represents 100% — shares are assigned by qualifying contribution, not guesswork.

Creator Share = Qualifying Value ÷ Total Qualifying Value × 100% of Pool

Illustrative example: four creators split one campaign pool purely by qualifying contribution.
